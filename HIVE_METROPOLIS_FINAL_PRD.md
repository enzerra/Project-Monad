# Historical Metropolis build specification — HIVE v2

**1–2 October 2026 · superseded by the current VIBE documentation**

This English summary distinguishes the planned build from verified delivery. The original full record remains in [Git history](https://github.com/enzerra/Project-Monad/blob/3bc68b2/HIVE_METROPOLIS_FINAL_PRD.md).

## Planned scope

Track 03: Social, Attention & Culture. The critical journey was a curated Gaming Short → timed Call → signed Monad commitment → post-close reveal → timestamped result → community discussion.

The design required real wallet consent, a relayer, a V2 contract, snapshot evidence and an honest explanation that the operator and YouTube API remain trusted. It proposed six/24-hour horizons, first-quarter entry windows, halfway-growth early close and a 30-minute result grace window.

The proposed reputation layer used early correct choices, sample sizes and a Wilson lower bound ranking. Proposed limits, activation targets, retention percentages, moderation and leaderboards were design goals, not measured adoption or implemented guarantees.

## Implemented evolution

The brand became VIBE. Discover now contains 20 curated Gaming, Sports and Crypto Shorts. Free VIBE credits use a separate server ledger with fixed 2× total returns, while the Monad flow remains a signed proof experience. Studio, account persistence and curated football Calls were added.

The deployed Monad contract is `HiveCallV2`. Two independent accounts completed commitment and reveal, followed by a verified resolution. See [transaction evidence](docs/MONAD.md).

Community persistence, verified reputation and a production leaderboard remain future work. The original no-credit scope and planned anti-abuse budgets must not be presented as current behavior.

## Submission requirements retained

Public source and an open-source license; setup and architecture documentation; external attribution; build-window history and AI disclosure; a working Monad deployment with transaction evidence; a publicly accessible demo no longer than three minutes; and a completed portal submission.

Choose one primary track. Sponsor bounties require their current individual conditions. Envio, Dynamic, Alchemy and Chainlink were considered during planning; this repository does not claim those integrations. Privy is actually used for authentication and signing, but the portal's bounty conditions still need review.
