The first Call has now resolved; verified reveal/result receipts are in [MONAD.md](MONAD.md). Future-tense recording notes below describe the earlier preparation state.

Screen recording with voice-over. Aim for 130–140 words per minute and leave time for taps and the wallet prompt. Record the result segment after the real evaluation, then edit the recording to three minutes or less.

| Time | Show | Voice-over |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:25 | Animated intro → Discover. | “Sometimes we discover a video before everyone else. We think: this is going to take off. Then the moment disappears into the feed. VIBE gives that early instinct a place to live. Discover a Short, make your call before the outcome is known, and return to see whether you were right.” |
| 0:25–0:50 | Gaming, Sports and Crypto Shorts; open a Call sheet. | “Discover puts one short video at the center. We curate twenty YouTube Shorts across Gaming, Sports, and Crypto, using the official player and creator attribution. Not every video needs a prediction. When a Call is available, one tap opens its target and deadline without losing the moment.” |
| 0:50–1:15 | Google login, Profile balance, ordinary VIBE Call. | “Sign in with Google and receive one thousand free VIBE credits. For a standard Call, choose Yes or No and an amount. A correct fifty-credit prediction returns one hundred in total. A wrong prediction loses fifty; a void returns it. These credits have no cash value and are stored in our server ledger.” |
| 1:15–1:50 | Monad Call; wallet signature; successful commit receipt and explorer. | “Here is our separate Monad proof flow. This Call asks whether a Short will reach its fixed views target within six hours. I select my answer and sign with my Privy wallet. Our relayer pays the Testnet gas. The contract records a commitment, and the receipt links directly to Monadscan. We have verified this flow with two independent Google accounts.” |
| 1:50–2:20 | Actual reveal/result transaction, snapshot timestamp, explorer. | “After the choice window closes, the operator reveals the signed choices. At evaluation, it submits a timestamped YouTube snapshot and the contract records the result. Views refresh periodically from the YouTube Data API; we never invent changes. The API and operator remain the trust boundary. If the source is unavailable within the result window, the Call is void.” |
| 2:20–3:00 | Studio preview/caption/hashtags; Profile; return to Discover. | “Creators also have a separate Studio. Preview a video, add its caption, category, and hashtags, then publish it to Discover. Profiles bring your videos and activity together. VIBE combines a familiar viewing experience with clear prediction rules and verifiable Monad records. Our prototype runs on Railway, with Privy authentication and Supabase persistence. The free-credit experience and the onchain proof experience are currently separate. VIBE: discover early, make your call, and see the outcome.” |
