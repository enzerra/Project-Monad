# VIBE — Three-minute demo script (English)

**Format:** screen-recorded product demo with voice-over. **Pace:** about 135 words per minute. Replace the example video and figures on screen with a live Call before recording; do not stage a result.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0:00–0:25** | VIBE animated intro → vertical Discover feed. | “We scroll past hundreds of videos every day. Sometimes we spot one before everyone else. But that early instinct disappears into the feed. VIBE gives it a place to live. Discover a moment, make a call before the outcome is known, and come back to see whether you were right.” |
| **0:25–0:55** | Swipe between YouTube Shorts in Gaming, Sports, and Crypto. Pause on a video with a Call; show another without one. | “Discover feels familiar: one short video at a time, with the creator and source always in view. The feed mixes videos worth watching with videos that have a clear prediction. Not every post needs a Call. You can simply watch, comment, or share. When a Call is available, one tap opens the question without losing the video.” |
| **0:55–1:25** | Open the Call sheet. Show current views, target, countdown, Yes/No, and VIBE balance. | “Here is the question: will this Short reach the target number of views within six hours? The starting count, target, deadline, and YouTube source are visible before you choose. Sign in with Google to receive free VIBE credits. Pick Yes or No, choose an amount, and confirm. One account gets one locked choice per Call.” |
| **1:25–1:55** | Confirm an available Call using a real account; show balance change and the live views indicator. | “Suppose I start with one thousand VIBE and use fifty on Yes. My balance becomes nine hundred and fifty. VIBE has no cash value: you cannot buy it, transfer it, or cash it out. The view count is fetched from the YouTube Data API and refreshed periodically. If YouTube has not reported a new number yet, VIBE keeps the last observed count instead of inventing movement.” |
| **1:55–2:25** | Open a different Call that has genuinely resolved. Show result, observed views, timestamp, and source. | “When the six-hour window ends, VIBE checks the first valid YouTube snapshot within the evaluation window. If the video reached the target, Yes wins. A correct fifty-VIBE choice receives one hundred VIBE in total: the original fifty plus fifty gained. A wrong choice loses the fifty. If the source cannot be verified, the Call is void and the fifty is returned. Every result shows the number and when it was observed.” |
| **2:25–3:00** | Show Predictions, Community, Profile, then the separate Upload Studio with preview, caption, category, and hashtags. Return to Discover and logo. | “Predictions brings open and finished Calls together. Community keeps the conversation near the moment, while your profile collects your activity and videos. Creators can also publish their own videos through a separate Studio: preview the clip, add a title, caption, category, and hashtags, then post it to Discover. Uploaded videos can stand on their own; a YouTube views Call is only shown when it has a verified source and a published rule. VIBE turns ‘I knew it early’ into a moment you can revisit. Discover early. Make your call. See the outcome.” |

**Recording check:** Use a real, currently open Call for the choice segment and a different, genuinely resolved Call for the result segment. Show the actual YouTube observation time. The free-credit Call flow is server-backed; do not describe it as an onchain Monad transaction.
