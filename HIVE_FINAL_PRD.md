# Historical product concept — HIVE v1

**1 October 2026 · superseded**

This is an English summary of the original planning document. For the current VIBE experience, use the [product specification](docs/PRODUCT.md), [source Call rules](VIBE_SOURCE_CALL_PRD.md) and [Monad integration](docs/MONAD.md).

## Original direction

Connect Discover, Prediction and Channel Community around one shared content object. A viewer could watch without making a prediction, open a curated Yes/No Call, discuss it and return to the result. The target audience included content explorers, trend observers, fans and curators.

The initial outcome template was a YouTube views target at six or 24 hours. Planned Calls fixed their source, baseline, target and deadlines before publication. The original design proposed closing entry in the first quarter of the horizon, timestamped source evidence and explicit void handling.

Reputation, Early & Right statistics, community moderation, notifications and an operator console were proposed. Human → Squad → Hive competition and the seven-phase Arena were separate future concepts.

## How the product evolved

VIBE now covers Gaming, Sports and Crypto; implements free server-held credits and selected football fixtures; and separates those credit Calls from the Monad commit–reveal proof flow. Community remains local and the proposed reputation system is not implemented.

Some early settlement rules and non-goals changed during implementation. Treat the current specifications as authoritative, not this concept summary. The full original document remains in [Git history](https://github.com/enzerra/Project-Monad/blob/3bc68b2/HIVE_FINAL_PRD.md).
